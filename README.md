# Ewing Tech Law Group (ETLG)

## Building the Future of Accessible Justice Through Artificial Intelligence

Ewing Tech Law Group (ETLG) is a next-generation legal technology initiative developed by **Thorbok Tech** under the broader strategic ecosystem of **Mason Ewing Corporation (USA)**.

ETLG explores the future of accessible justice through artificial intelligence, AI Attorneys, TB AI, accessibility-first systems, future investigative technologies, NONS investigative robotics, and the Memory Die Research Initiative.

## Project Overview

ETLG is designed as a human-centered legal technology ecosystem focused on expanding access to justice for underserved populations, including individuals facing poverty, disability, language limitations, geographic isolation, or lack of legal resources.

The project combines human legal expertise, intelligent assistance systems, accessibility-first design, and future investigative research programs.
